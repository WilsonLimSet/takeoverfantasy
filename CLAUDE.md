## Social posts
`src/lib/social.ts` (data + captions), `src/app/api/card/social` (1080x1350 cards; links must be signed via `cardUrl`), `src/lib/socialPush.ts` (runs from the news cron at 05:00 and 15:00 UTC). Two kinds only, to avoid spam (~170 slideshows a season):
- **Daily waiver wire** (15:00 UTC run): one carousel, each player shown once: cover ("Today's waiver wire", led by last night's best pickup; the brand name isn't the hook while it's new), more waiver heroes (<50% rostered, finished games only), trending adds/drops, minutes watch (minutes vs last season), injury pickups (real OUTs with a pickup, plus players back), and a call to action.
- **Transactions** (max one a day, from the twice-daily run): a 50%+ rostered player moved or cut. Injuries post only from the 10-minute check (below); the twice-daily run posted them hours late.
No posts about role changes on stars or "still out" follow-ups (Wilson: obvious, priced-in news is not insightful). Branding says "your fantasy team", not ESPN; "% rostered across ESPN leagues" is fine as the data source. The slideshow goes to Instagram only; Threads and X get three standalone posts instead ("Drop your roster below" an hour after the adds post; replies to all three are answered by `src/lib/replyBot.ts` via Postroom's reply hook `/api/reply`), "Who ESPN managers are adding right now" and "Last night's waiver heroes" two hours later (on Threads, single-topic posts got 400-700 views, the slideshow ~40). Posts go to Postroom (Instagram, Threads, X), the owner's Telegram (/posts), Telegram users with news on (/nonews), and a daily email digest. **Takes** (`src/lib/waiverTakes.ts`): hold/stream/stash/pass calls from the value model on the players everyone's talking about, pinned per day (`social:takesDay:<date>`). Claude (Sonnet, `src/lib/socialEditor.ts`) writes the words and picks which take leads each post; numbers it quotes must be in the facts or the template is used. Satire (`writeSatire`): a 60%+ rostered player who stank last night (24+ min, under 55% of his usual points, or 30% shooting on 15+ shots) gets a roast card 5h after the morning run; only the basketball is roasted, the fantasy call is real, and nothing posts unless the numbers check out. Learning (`src/lib/socialStats.ts`): Postroom reads each post's numbers (`/api/v1/stats`); formats are scored against each platform's median, the editor reads the best and worst posts before writing, the morning carousel (takes vs slideshow) shifts to the winner two days in three, and a single format under 40% of the best runs every other day. Instagram: the morning carousel, a take over a big photo 3h later, satire 5h, a stat headline 7h (singles also on Threads/X). Breaking alerts (`postBreaking`) also post publicly within 10 minutes as a Shams-style card, max 2 a day, 50%+ rostered and out at least two weeks (Wilson: shorter absences aren't big news). Stars (70%+) newly ruled out of tonight's game get an "Out tonight" card with the teammate who gets his minutes (`postTonight`, max 3 a day): posted only if there are 20+ minutes to tip-off, published right away, and Postroom drops it if it isn't out before tip-off (`expiresAt`). Rest, load management and personal absences never post. Box-score minutes are saved daily (`social:minutes:<date>`) for a future minutes-trends slide.

## Player values
`src/lib/value.ts` is the model (Wilson's rules): chance he plays × minutes × points per minute. Windows (streams) count only current injury status; rest of season adds durability. Minutes follow recent box-score minutes (`social:minutes:<date>`) with temporary-role flags when a same-position regular is out with a return date. Rebounds/assists/stocks trust this season fast; scoring leans on the projection longer and sets the floor/ceiling. No penalty for bad teams. Durability comes from game logs (`src/lib/healthHistory.ts`, KV `value:health`, last 3 seasons, rotation seasons only): long absences (10+ games) are one-off injuries and don't count against him; many short absences mark him "in-and-out". Teammate dependence (`dependsOn`): for each injured regular on his team with a return date, the minutes he gains, measured from recent games with vs without the teammate and blended with #47's expected split (`shareMinutes`) while samples are thin; values before and after the return date use those minutes. The AI's news reads (`src/lib/autoTakes.ts` role multipliers on a take) adjust the starting expectation in `perGameCore`, then fade as games pile up; autoTakes bills ANTHROPIC_API_KEY (Claude credits) first, then OPENAI_API_KEY, then AI Gateway. Nightly fantasy points are saved as `social:fpts:<date>` for a future usage (points per minute) split. The per-game core (`src/lib/valueCore.ts`) is shared with the posts (`fppg`). The daily run saves predictions (`value:pred:<date>`) and scores them against the box scores (`value:err:<date>`). Day-to-day players with a report return date count as out until it (ESPN's day-to-day covers multi-day sprains); the games count and the moves engine use the same rule (`src/lib/inseason/availability.ts`: return date first, back from it even off IR, the tag only without a date). Used by the reply bot and /move; the in-season moves engine (`src/lib/inseason/streaming.ts`) still has its own `expectedFppg`.

## Breaking alerts
`src/lib/breaking.ts`, cron `/api/cron/breaking` every 10 minutes (`?dry=1` previews). Diffs ESPN's injury report (fetched fresh) against `breaking:status`; a 40%+ rostered player newly OUT for 3+ days triggers a Telegram alert (every linked user with news on, plus owner chats) and, for 70%+ rostered players, an email (max one per person per day). Pickups: teammates gaining 2+ minutes in the shared split (`shareMinutes`), ranked by value while he's out, under 60% rostered. Paying users with a connected league also see whether the pickup is free in their league (with the ESPN add link) and whether the injured player is theirs. Two sources: the report, and ESPN player news read by Claude (`src/lib/ai.ts` chain: ANTHROPIC_API_KEY first) for 40%+ rostered players whose `lastNewsDate` moved (one light players feed per check, at most 15 news reads), flagged only for extended absences (more than two games). Claude then picks the best pickup from the teammates the minutes split flags, with a one-line reason. Report-sourced alerts need a return date (undated "out" flips with game-time calls; undated long absences come through the news path). One alert per player per 14 days (`breaking:sent:<id>` holds the time). State is saved at the end of a run and news only counts as seen once Claude has read it, so failures retry instead of dropping alerts. When the injured player is on a paying user's roster, the alert adds the best free agents in their league for the open IR spot (`src/lib/backupPlan.ts`: ranked by points over today through next matchup week). `/backup` on Telegram sends the standing shortlist by position. The This week page leads with the answer (`EdgeCard`): points the moves add and which days they fill, the matchup's projected final with and without them (points so far + best lineups for the days left, both sides), what the core list costs (the same search allowed to drop anyone outside the best starters), and warnings (an IR player no longer out blocks ESPN adds). Each move shows a day strip (games that make the lineup vs land on a full day) and the moves are listed in the order to make them; the 23:00 UTC refresh sends a Telegram reminder on days a move is due after tonight's games. All-core rosters get no daily moves message (the engine never drops core players, and no moves means no message).

## Turn of the week
In a matchup's last 2 game days, `buildStreamingPlan` adds `bridge`: free agents (not waivers) who start on the days left and in next week's first 2 days, each with his best drop, ranked by lineup points over both stretches. A pick must add at least the minimum gain this week too: if the drop also plays the last day, keeping him and swapping after the week gets next week's games just as well. Shown on This week as `BridgePanel`.

## Trades
`src/lib/trades.ts`. Free checker at `/trade?give=…&get=…` (public, shareable); paid finder on the Trades tab (`/api/inseason/trades`). The checker gives from your roster and gets from the other rosters when a league is connected (`?all=1` for any player). The finder tries 1-for-1, 2-for-1 and 1-for-2 (dropping your weakest healthy player to make room), sending only players on your trade block (`trades:block:<email>:<league>:<team>:<season>`, empty = anyone). Your side is judged on rest-of-season value (`ros`, durability counted); anyone below free-agent level counts as one, and each open spot is worth a free agent. Their side is judged on what managers see (last 5 games from `social:fpts`, the season, preseason projection) on a curve that favors stars, so offers must look fair to them on box scores. Buy low = league mates' players 15%+ below what their role says (never ones who are out long or sit often); sell high = yours worth more to others than to you (hot streak, role that ends when a teammate returns, sits often).
